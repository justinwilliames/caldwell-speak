## Statement of method, not a warranty

Earlier revisions carried the sentence *"no real-person likeness; not modelled on a
third-party copyrighted character"* as a flat assertion. That was written when the
cast were stylised robots with lens faces. **The v8 cast are nine photoreal human
faces**, and the same sentence over photoreal output from a model trained on
photographs would be an untested claim presented as a verified one. It is therefore
replaced by a description of what was actually done and what was and was not checked:

- **What ran.** Image-to-image generation from this project's own prior masters for
  each character, under prompts authored in this repository. No third-party image,
  photograph or artwork was supplied as an input, so there is no external rights
  chain in the inputs.
- **What was instructed.** Every prompt names an invented character with an invented
  age, build and background. No prompt names, describes or references any real
  person, living or dead, and no prompt requests the likeness of any public figure,
  actor or third-party copyrighted character.
- **What was checked.** The outputs were reviewed by the project maintainers and no
  output was recognised as depicting an identifiable individual.
- **What was NOT checked.** No reverse-image search, facial-recognition search or
  systematic likeness clearance has been performed against any database of real
  people. **Record this as an inability to exclude an incidental resemblance, not as
  clearance.** A generative model can produce a face that coincidentally resembles a
  real person, and nothing here rules that out.
- **If you believe a portrait resembles a specific real person,** open an issue and
  the asset will be regenerated.

## AI-generation disclosure and content credentials

The renders returned by the generator carry a signed **C2PA** manifest and a **SynthID**
watermark, declaring IPTC `digitalSourceType: trainedAlgorithmicMedia`.

- **Archival originals**, byte-identical to what the generator returned and with the
  signed manifest intact, are kept in `generated-images/masters-archive/`. Note these
  are **JPEG** data despite historically being written to `.png` filenames; the archive
  stores them under their true extension.
- **Derived assets** (the eye-registered crops and the lip-sync frames) are re-encoded,
  which no signed manifest can survive. Each derived PNG therefore carries `tEXt`
  metadata recording the generator, the model id, `digitalSourceType`, and a pointer
  back to its archival original.
- Verify with: `python3 -c "print(open('<file>','rb').read().count(b'trainedAlgorithmicMedia'))"`

## Method

Each master was generated image-to-image from the project's **own prior master**
for that character (never a third-party image, and never another artist's work),
under the house standard recorded in `design/FACTORY-STYLE.md`. Reference images
used are therefore all previously-generated assets from this same repository, so
there is no external rights chain in the inputs.

**Reproducibility limit, stated plainly:** `gemini-3-pro-image` accepts no seed, so
no render in this cast is bit-reproducible. Re-running a prompt produces a different
face. The archival masters are therefore the only authoritative copy of each asset.

## The pipeline, and how to check it

Every step lives in `scripts/` and runs from a clean checkout:

| Step | Script |
|---|---|
| Generate a character (needs `GEMINI_API_KEY`) | `scripts/cast-generate.py [drone …]` |
| Archive true-format masters, embed provenance | `scripts/cast-provenance.py` |
| Register the cast on their glowing irises | `scripts/cast-align.py [version]` |
| Enforce the cast laws — **exits non-zero on failure** | `scripts/cast-gate.py [version]` |
| Write the asset record | `scripts/cast-manifest.py [version]` |

`generated-images/cast-manifest.json` records, per character: the archival master's
SHA-256, true format and whether its C2PA/SynthID credential is intact; the derived
asset's SHA-256 and whether it declares AI provenance; and a hash of the exact prompt
block that produced it. **Because renders are not reproducible, this manifest is the
accountability mechanism** — if an asset changes without the manifest changing,
something happened off the record.

The prompts themselves are in `scripts/cast-generate.py` and are the reproducible part
of this record.

Lip-sync frames were generated as a 3x2 sprite sheet per character (six 512px
cells) from the approved master, cropped, then registered with OpenCV
`findTransformECC` (MOTION_EUCLIDEAN) and downsampled to 362px.

### Rev 9.1 — Pulsar re-centred (2026-08-04)

Pulsar's frame set shipped with his head **10.4px left** of the 362px canvas
centre (2.9% of the width), which reads in the squircle as a fat gap on his
right. Measured three ways — head bilateral-symmetry axis (−10.4px), head-bbox
centroid (−10.0px), and left/right silhouette gap (16px vs 35px, −9.5px).

**The masters were not at fault.** Every master measures within ±0.6% of centre,
Pulsar's `assets/readme/pulsar.png` included (+0.8%). The miss entered in the
sprite-cell crop that derives the 362px frames, and only for Pulsar — the other
eight frame sets measure −1.0% to +0.4%. Nebula's symmetry metric reads −21px,
but that is intrinsic design asymmetry (swept quiff, single antenna ball); a
visual check confirms she is centred.

**Correction:** all six Pulsar frames translated **+10px right** as a pure
integer pixel copy, with column 0 replicated into the vacated 10px strip. No
resampling, so no softening; the strip is background (only 13 rows have any
subject at the left edge, and those sit outside the squircle's clipped corner).
Result: Pulsar now measures −0.5px (−0.14%), the best-centred of the nine.
Inter-frame registration is unchanged — phase correlation of every frame against
its `mouth-0` still returns (0,0) for all nine sets.

Regression cover: `scripts/cast-check.sh` **check 11** now gates both portrait
centring (per-drone ratified baseline, ±3px) and lip-sync frame registration.
Both arms are negative-tested.

Frames live in `macos/Pulsar/Sources/Resources/`. Pre-Rev-8 masters are retained at `design/drones/pre-rev8/` and the original
pre-machined cast at `design/drones/before/`. Meridian's Rev 9 master was
generated as four FULL re-renders rather than masked edits, after compounding
inpaints produced a chest emblem and glow that read as applied rather than
rendered.

## Prompts

The full prompt text for each generation is preserved in the build scratchpad
alongside each attempt (`prompt-<drone>-<attempt>.txt`). The governing standard,
including every ruling that shaped these designs, is `design/FACTORY-STYLE.md`
(revisions 1-8, with the rationale for each change recorded in the git history).

## Characters are fictional

The nine drones are invented cognitive frames — names, voices and faces given to
sub-agents so a user can tell concurrent work apart by ear and on screen. None is
a real person, an employee, or a portrait of anyone.
